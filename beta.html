What is actually useful to tell us

Bugs are the easy part. The things worth more than a crash report:

  • →Where it made you tap more than it should have. Count them if you can stand it.

  • →What you still do outside the app: on paper, in texts, in another app, in your head.
  • →What you would have to explain to a new guy before he could use it.
