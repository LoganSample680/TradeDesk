The short version
  • We do not sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. There are no ad networks and no third party analytics trackers in this app.
  • Location logging is opt in and it is on a leash. It runs inside the working hours you set, it can be switched off from inside the app at any time, and a crew member has to accept it on their own phone before it starts.
  • Your business records are yours. Clients, estimates, jobs, hours, invoices. We hold them so the app works on all of your devices, and we do not mine them.
  • You can delete the whole account from inside the app. Settings, Danger zone, Delete account. It removes the login too, not just the records.
  • A crew member's hours belong to the business that employed them. If crew delete their account we remove the person and their location history, and leave the employer's payroll record standing with the name taken off.

1Who we are

TradeDesk is software for trade contractors: painting, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, landscaping and general contracting. It runs the job from the first phone call to getting paid.

This policy covers the TradeDesk iPhone app, the TradeDesk web app, and the public pages a contractor sends to their customers (proposal signing, intake forms, timesheet approval). Throughout, "you" means the person using TradeDesk, and "your business" means the contracting business whose account you are working in.

Questions about anything here go to privacy@tradedesk.app.

3Location and motion, in detail

This is the part that deserves the most words, so it gets them.

What turns it on

Nothing happens until you switch tracking on inside TradeDesk and grant iOS location permission. If you choose "While Using the App", drives that happen with your phone in your pocket will not be logged, which is why the app asks for "Always". You can change your mind in iOS Settings at any time and the app keeps working without it, minus the automatic logging.

What it actually records, and when

  • It follows the working hours you set in the app. Outside that window the app is not asking for your position.
  • Most of the day it uses the motion coprocessor, not GPS: the phone tells the app "this person started driving", and only then does the app take fixes. This is why the app can log a drive without draining the battery.
  • While a drive is running it records the route so the mileage log has real miles rather than a straight line.
  • When the phone is parked or standing still, the app takes a position roughly every 30 minutes so a manager can see a crew member is still on site.
  • It records arrival and departure at addresses already saved in your account (job sites, the shop, your home if you have marked it). It is not building a map of the other places you go.

Home, and time that is nobody's business

Time at a place you have marked as home only counts as work when the app is actually open and it falls outside the working day. Sitting at home in the evening with the app closed produces no work record.

Turning it off

Location tracking can be switched off inside TradeDesk without deleting anything, and revoked entirely in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Location Services. The location history already recorded stays until you delete it or delete the account.

Who can see it

  • You, always, on your own time log and mileage log.
  • The owner and any manager of the business you are clocked into, and only for the account you are working in. A manager sees the live crew map, and current day positions for people who are still clocked in.
  • Nobody else. Not other businesses on TradeDesk, not advertisers, not data brokers. Location data is never sold or licensed, at any price, to anyone.

If you have both a crew login at an employer and your own business on TradeDesk, the two are walled off from each other. Work you log for your own business is not visible to your employer, and the reverse.

4If you are crew, here is exactly what your employer sees

You should not have to guess at this, so it is written out.

  • Your position while you are clocked in during the working hours the business has set, including the route of your work drives.
  • When you arrived at a job site and when you left, and the hours that produces.
  • Your phone's battery level and whether location permission is on, so a manager can tell "he is off the map" from "his phone died".
  • The jobs, notes, photos and time entries you record in the app.

What your employer does not get: your position outside the set working hours, your position when you are not clocked in, anything from your personal apps, your messages, your contacts, or your microphone.

Consent is yours to give, and to withdraw

Tracking on a personal phone takes two separate yeses: the business has to grant it for your account, and you have to accept it on your own phone. If you decline, tracking stays off on that device. There is no covert mode and there is no way for a business to switch it on behind you.

5Your clients' details

When a contractor enters a customer's name, address, phone and email into TradeDesk, that information belongs to the contractor's business. We hold and process it on their instructions so the app can send a proposal, schedule a visit and raise an invoice. We do not market to your customers, and we do not use their details for anything except running the features you asked for.

If you are a homeowner who received a proposal, an intake form or an invoice from a contractor using TradeDesk, the contractor is the one who holds your details. Ask them to correct or remove them, and they can do it from inside the app. You can also write to us and we will pass it on.

7What we never do

  • We never sell or rent personal information, and we never share it for cross context behavioural advertising. Under California law that means no "sale" and no "share".
  • We do not run ad networks, ad SDKs or third party analytics trackers in the app.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not use your business records or your crew's location to train machine learning models.
  • We do not read the audio from dictation. It is transcribed on your iPhone and we only ever receive the text you keep.

8How long we keep it, and how you delete it

We keep your records for as long as the account is open, because that is what the account is for: last year's mileage log is what you hand your accountant. Location pings older than a rolling window are thinned automatically so the app is not hoarding minute by minute position forever.

Deleting your account, from inside the app

Open TradeDesk, go to Settings, Danger zone, Delete account, and confirm. What happens next depends on which kind of account it is, and the app tells you which one you have before you confirm.

  • A business owner: we delete the login and everything in the business, clients, estimates, jobs, invoices, expenses, mileage, location history, files, and we unlink the crew so nobody is left pointing at a business that no longer exists. Crew keep their own logins.
  • A crew member: we delete the login, the link to the employer, and your location and device history. The hours you worked stay in your employer's payroll record with your name taken off them, because those are their books and they have their own legal obligation to keep them.

Deletion is immediate and it is not reversible. Copies can persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they roll off, and we keep the minimum needed for tax and accounting purposes on payments already taken.

If you cannot reach the app for any reason, write to privacy@tradedesk.app from the address on the account and we will do it for you.

9Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Passwords are stored hashed and we cannot read them. Access to an account's records is enforced at the database itself with row level security, so one business cannot read another's rows even if the app asked it to. Employee access to money and estimates is gated by the permissions the owner sets.

No system is perfect. If we ever discover a breach affecting your personal information we will tell affected users and the relevant regulators as the law requires, and we will say what happened rather than issue a shrug.

10Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, export it, or delete it. Most of this you can do yourself: the app exports your records, every record is editable, and account deletion is a button.

California residents have the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

People in the UK and EEA: our lawful bases are performance of the contract (running the app you signed up for), legitimate interests (keeping it secure and working), consent (location tracking and notifications, which you can withdraw at any time), and legal obligation (tax records). You may object, restrict, request portability, and complain to your supervisory authority.

Ask at privacy@tradedesk.app. We answer within 30 days and we do not charge for it.

11Children

TradeDesk is a tool for running a business and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If a child's information reached us, write to us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

If we change something that matters, particularly anything about location, sharing or retention, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before it takes effect. We will not quietly widen what we collect and hope nobody rereads the page.
